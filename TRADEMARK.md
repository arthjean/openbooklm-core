# Trademark and Naming Policy

The OpenbookLM source code is licensed under Apache-2.0. **The name "OpenbookLM",
the name "StriveX", and the associated logos and visual identity are not covered
by that license.** Apache-2.0 section 6 grants no trademark rights, and this
document states how the marks may and may not be used.

## What you may do without asking

- State factually that your product, fork or service "is built on OpenbookLM",
  "uses OpenbookLM", or "is compatible with OpenbookLM", provided the statement
  is accurate and does not suggest affiliation, sponsorship or endorsement.
- Use the name in prose, documentation, talks, articles, comparisons and academic
  work.
- Keep the name in unmodified copies of the software you redistribute.
- Use the name in a package, image or repository name that clearly reads as a
  third-party integration rather than an official distribution, for example
  `openbooklm-terraform-module` or `myorg-openbooklm-helm-chart`.

## What requires written permission

- Naming a modified distribution, hosted service or commercial product
  "OpenbookLM", or a name a reasonable user could confuse with it.
- Using the logos or visual identity in your own product UI, marketing, app store
  listing or domain name.
- Registering a domain, social account, organization or package namespace whose
  primary element is "OpenbookLM".
- Any use that implies official status: "official", "certified", "powered by the
  OpenbookLM team", or similar.

## Forks

Forking is explicitly permitted by the license and encouraged. A substantially
modified fork must carry its own name so that users can tell whose software they
are running and whom to hold responsible for it. Keep an accurate attribution
line such as "based on OpenbookLM" rather than reusing the mark as your product
name.

## Hosted services

Apache-2.0 permits hosting a competing service built on this core. When you do,
name your service distinctly. Describing it as "built on OpenbookLM" is fine;
calling it "OpenbookLM Cloud", "Managed OpenbookLM" or similar is not.

The hosted service operated by StriveX is the only one that may present itself as
the official OpenbookLM service.
